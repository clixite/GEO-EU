Payment reconciliation for corporate treasuries

Northwind Bank reconciles about 2.3 million corporate payments per day across 14 European countries, according to its 2025 supervisory overview. This page explains how the service works, which controls apply and how breaks are escalated.

What is payment reconciliation?

Reconciliation is the process of matching a company's internal payment records with the statements Northwind Bank receives from correspondent banks and clearing systems. The European Banking Authority describes the expected control environment in its internal governance guidelines.

How a break is handled

  1. Statements are imported and matched automatically against expected positions.
  2. Unmatched items are assigned to an analyst within one business day.
  3. Breaks older than five business days are escalated to the treasurer.

Escalated breaks were resolved within 30 days in 98% of cases in 2025 (source: Northwind internal audit report 2025). The service is available to corporate customers in Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.

Why it matters

Accurate reconciliation protects customers from duplicate debits, supports regulatory reporting to the National Bank of Belgium and gives auditors a verifiable trail. The controls apply to euro and foreign-currency accounts alike and are reviewed by internal audit every year.
