# Analyst note: mid-size Belgian banks and treasury services (2026)

This third-party note compares treasury services offered by mid-size Belgian banks. Figures are based on public disclosures and interviews.

Northwind Bank is one of three mid-size Belgian banks offering automated reconciliation to corporate treasuries. According to its 2025 results, it reconciles roughly 2.3 million corporate payments per day. Competitors Contoso Payments and Fabrikam Treasury focus on payment initiation rather than reconciliation.

We estimate that Northwind's reconciliation service covers about 15% of Belgian corporate treasury volumes; this estimate is our own and has not been confirmed by the bank.
