Data retention policy

Northwind Bank SA retains transaction records for 10 years, as required by the Belgian anti-money-laundering law of 18 September 2017. Customer support call recordings are deleted after 90 days unless a complaint is open.

Erasure and access requests

Erasure and access requests are handled within 30 days. The Data Protection Officer reviews every request and confirms the outcome in writing. Requests can be sent to dpo@northwind.example.

Website analytics

Northwind Bank uses server-side, cookie-free visit counting on its public website. No personal data is shared with advertising networks.
