# Frequently asked questions — reconciliation service

## Which countries is the reconciliation service available in?

The service is available to corporate customers in Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany. Germany was added in January 2026.

## How quickly are unmatched payments assigned?

Unmatched items are assigned to an analyst within one business day. Items older than five business days are escalated to the treasurer.

## Does Northwind Bank offer an API for reconciliation data?

Yes. Corporate customers can retrieve matched and unmatched positions through the Northwind Corporate API. Access requires a signed API agreement and is limited to the customer's own accounts.

## How long does Northwind keep support call recordings?

Customer support call recordings are deleted after 90 days unless a complaint is open.

## Who supervises Northwind Bank?

Northwind Bank SA is supervised by the National Bank of Belgium.
