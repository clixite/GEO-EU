# Northwind Bank reports 2025 results and expands reconciliation service to Germany

Brussels, 12 March 2026 — Northwind Bank SA today published its results for the financial year 2025.

## Key figures

- Net banking income of 412 million EUR, up 6% on 2024.
- 2.3 million corporate payments reconciled per day on average in 2025.
- Reconciliation service now available in 14 European countries after the launch in Germany in January 2026.
- 1,860 employees at year end, of whom 1,120 are based in Belgium.

## Statement

"Corporate treasurers expect their bank to reconcile payments the same day and to explain every break. In 2025 we resolved 98% of escalated breaks within 30 days," said Anna Peeters, Head of Payments.

## About Northwind Bank

Northwind Bank SA is a Belgian bank founded in 1998, headquartered in Brussels and supervised by the National Bank of Belgium. It serves corporate customers in Belgium, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, France and Germany.
