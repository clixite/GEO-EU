# Product

## Identity

- **Name:** Evidentia (working product name; trademark clearance pending — ADR-0001).
- **Positioning:** Governed generative visibility for European organisations.
- **Short description:** A governed GEO/AEO platform and Agent Skill that turns
  an organisation's own documented knowledge into content AI answer engines can
  retrieve and cite, gates every publication behind evidence checks and human
  approval, and measures visibility with defensible statistics.
- **Long description:** Evidentia makes authoritative organisations easier for
  humans and AI systems to discover, understand, verify, cite and trust. It
  ingests first-party sources into a provenance-tracked knowledge base, extracts
  claims and entities, retrieves evidence with hybrid search, drafts content that
  cites its passages, verifies every claim deterministically, applies
  machine-readable governance policies (which model may see which data; when a
  human must approve), records every action in a hash-chained ledger, publishes
  with a visible AI disclosure and a signed provenance manifest, and measures
  brand mention and citation across AI engines with repeated sampling and
  confidence intervals. It ships as a self-hosted platform (core, CLI,
  governance console), a public website and a Claude Code Agent Skill.
- **Tagline:** Be cited on evidence, not tricks.
- **Vocabulary:** Knowledge (sources, documents, claims, entities) · Evidence ·
  Readiness · Observatory · Gate · Approval · Register · Ledger · Manifest · Policy.
- **Tone:** precise, restrained, European, evidence-first; no superlatives
  without sources; no compliance badges.
- **Mandatory mark:** *Built and maintained by Clixite SRL — Belgium.*

## Core principle

TRUSTED KNOWLEDGE → STRUCTURED UNDERSTANDING → EVIDENCE-GROUNDED CONTENT →
GOVERNANCE GATES → HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY → DISTRIBUTION → AI DISCOVERABILITY →
MEASUREMENT → CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT. Never spam, fabricated expertise, fake
citations, hidden text, mass low-quality pages or manipulation.
